# Mastery update: 5–10 player phone playtest

This is a session plan, not completed research. No participant results have been collected by the build or automated tests.

Use the current APK on Android, or a native iPhone build when a Mac/signing setup is available. Record the build, device and control mode in SESSION.md. Recruit a mix of occasional mobile players and players who enjoy difficult dexterity games. Observe each person separately for about 15 minutes.

## Observe first

1. Let the player launch the game and use the tutorial without coaching. Ask them to try Classic level 1. Record first intentional movement, first target, their first loss explanation and any help they need.
2. After their first full run ends, leave the choice to them. Record whether they retry, advance, explore or stop before asking any replay question.
3. Ask what the three stars mean and where they would look for their best result. Record their words. If they have not finished, do not infer that they understand rewards from seeing empty stars.
4. Let them pursue another run as they choose. Record whether they notice a brass coin, deliberately detour toward it, and can distinguish it from a hole. Note the tradeoff they describe.
5. Let them open Daily and Cabinet. Ask what they expect to stay the same on a retry, when Daily changes, and how a style unlocks. Record confusion about control-specific records or style handling.
6. Let them choose a finale from the grid. Check whether they read the briefing, recognize the warning, and can explain the eventual hit. A novice struggling with the base controls should not be treated as evidence that level 50 is unfair.
7. Try Infinite. Record the height reached and whether a lighter section, rush or special encounter actually occurred. Do not ask them to evaluate a section they never reached.

## Time-star tuning

Use successful active times from the result screen, separated by board and control mode. Record attempts and whether coins were pursued. Keep assisted or staged completions separate. Tune targets from repeated real successes: the time star should require deliberate improvement while remaining achievable with normal touch input. The current formula is an initial budget, not a measured human performance benchmark.

## Questions after free choice

- What made you want to continue or stop?
- Which loss felt deserved, and which felt surprising? Show the exact moment if possible.
- Which goal would you pursue next, if any?
- What was unclear in the grid, results, Daily or Cabinet?

Use SESSION.md for raw observations and RESULTS.md for a synthesis after sessions. Record next-day return only if it actually happens; stated intention is not return behavior. Do not invent participants, averages or completion rates.
